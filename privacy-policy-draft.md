## What we collect

**Nothing.** Blocks & Pets does not collect, transmit, or share any personal
information.

- No account or login is required to play.
- We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or device
  identifiers.
- The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

## What stays on your device

Your game progress (levels, pets, settings, streaks) is stored **only on your
device** using on-device storage. It is never uploaded to our servers — we
operate no servers for this game. Deleting the app deletes this data.

## Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play
Store. We never see or handle your payment information. Store receipts used for
purchase restore are handled on-device.

## Notifications

If enabled, reminders and notifications are scheduled locally on your device.
No notification data leaves the device.

## Children

Blocks & Pets is a casual puzzle game suitable for general audiences. Because we
collect no personal information from anyone, including children, there is no
children's data to protect, retain, or delete.
