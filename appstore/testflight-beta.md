# Tanks & Kings — TestFlight Beta Test Information

Saved 2026-10-14 from chat. Paste into App Store Connect → Tanks & Kings → TestFlight tab → Test Information (left sidebar), then Save. This is the one-time step blocking the external beta group.

## Beta App Description

```
Tanks & Kings is a side-scrolling idle battler. Command chibi tanks from five kingdoms, battle through endless waves and boss fights, evolve your tanks through five tiers up to Ascendant, unlock godskins, and climb the Glory ranks, Tower, and Guild boss modes.

This beta tests core battle gameplay, progression and saving, in-game purchases (sandbox), and performance on iPhone. All content is playable; purchases use Apple's sandbox and are not charged.
```

## What to Test

```
Please play Campaign battles across several waves and boss fights. Test the battle skills (Charge/Missile, Parry, Leap, Repair, Ultimate), tank evolution, and the Tower, Glory, and Guild modes. Report any crashes, visual glitches, sound issues, or performance problems, and note your device model.
```

## After saving

- The external group can be submitted for beta review — the first external build goes through Apple's beta review (usually ~24h); after that, new builds added to the group mostly go live without another full review.
