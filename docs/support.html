Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by the Apple App Store. If a purchase doesn't appear in the game, open the Royal Shop and tap Restore Purchases — your purchases are tied to your Apple ID and can be restored on any of your devices. For refunds, please contact Apple Support, as developers cannot process App Store refunds directly.

Game progress

Your progress is saved on your device and backed up to your own iCloud account when iCloud is enabled, so it follows you when you reinstall or move to a new device signed into the same Apple ID.
