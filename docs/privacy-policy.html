Privacy Policy — Tanks & Kings

Effective date: November 5, 2026

Developer: Jay Lee

Contact: jaylee8506@gmail.com

What we collect

We do not collect personal information. Tanks & Kings has no accounts and

requires no login, and we operate no servers for this game.

  • We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, or photos.
  • The app contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and does not

track you across apps or websites.

  • Two services described below — purchase processing and iCloud backup —

involve data handled by Apple and by our purchase provider under their own

privacy policies. Neither gives us your identity.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by the Apple App Store. We never see

or handle your payment information.

To verify purchases and let you restore them on a new device, our purchase

provider, RevenueCat, receives your App Store receipt, the product

identifiers you purchased, and an anonymous app user ID generated by the app.

This identifier is not linked to your name or Apple ID by us. RevenueCat

stores your purchase history on its servers so the Restore Purchases feature

works; we can see aggregate purchase records but not who you are.

Restorable (non-consumable) purchases — the Starter Pack and Season Pass

Premium — are restored from that purchase history. Consumable gem packs are

spent as you use them and are not restored, which is standard App Store

practice.

Cloud backup (iCloud)

If you are signed in to iCloud on your device, your game progress is

automatically backed up to your own iCloud account using Apple's iCloud

Key-Value storage, so your progress survives deleting the app and syncs

across your own devices signed in to the same Apple ID.

This backup goes from your device to your iCloud account. We cannot access

it, and it is never sent to any server of ours — we operate none. You can

remove it at any time from your device's iCloud settings. If iCloud is off

or unavailable, progress is stored only on your device and deleting the app

deletes it.

Fonts

The app's display fonts are loaded from Google Fonts when the game opens,

which means your device contacts Google's servers (which receive your IP

address and device user agent, as with any web request). No game data is

sent. If the fonts cannot be loaded — for example, with no connection on

first launch — the game falls back to system fonts.

Notifications

If enabled, reminders and notifications are scheduled locally on your device.

No notification data leaves the device.

Children

Tanks & Kings is a side-scrolling idle battler game suitable for general

audiences. We do not collect personal information from anyone, including

children; the purchase and iCloud flows above use anonymous identifiers and

your own Apple account only.
